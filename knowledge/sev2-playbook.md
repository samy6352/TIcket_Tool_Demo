# Sample Support Playbook (training only)

This playbook is fictional. It exists only for the Service Desk Helper demo and is not a Microsoft or customer policy.

## Severity 2 update cadence

Sev 2 cases receive an initial response within 1 hour of being logged. After that, the owner posts a progress update every 60 minutes until the case is mitigated or resolved.

## Escalation

If there is no workable mitigation for a Sev 2 case, contact the on-call lead. Record the escalation time in the ticket notes.

## Ticket status meanings

- Investigating: an owner is actively working the case.
- Waiting on customer: the team needs information from the customer before continuing.
- Resolved: a fix or workaround has been confirmed.
